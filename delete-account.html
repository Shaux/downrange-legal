Delete your Downrange account
Downrange Account deletion
Package com.downrange.analyzer

1.In the app

Open Settings → Sync → Delete cloud account and confirm. This permanently deletes your synced data, your target photos in the cloud and your sign-in. Data stored on your device is not affected.

2.If you cannot open the app

Email info.downrangebalistics@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my Downrange account". We will delete your account and its data and confirm by reply, within 30 days.

3.What is deleted

  • Your synced groups, loads, rifles, round log, sessions, saved analyses and target presets
  • Your target photos stored in the cloud
  • Your sign-in record (the email and Google identifier we hold)

Anonymous usage statistics are not linked to your account. To have them deleted too, say so in your email and include the install identifier shown in Settings. Purchase records are held by Google Play and RevenueCat under their own policies.
