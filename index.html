In short

Downrange works fully offline, and by default your data stays on your device. Your photos, shot groups, loads and settings are stored on your phone. There is no advertising, no tracking and nothing is sold.

Sync is optional and off until you turn it on: it keeps a copy in your own account so your data follows you across devices. Subscriptions are handled by Google Play and RevenueCat.

1.What Downrange is

Downrange is a target-analysis tool for shooting-sports practice. You photograph or manually mark a shot group, and the app computes group statistics (spread, dispersion, point-of-impact offset and related metrics) on the device. It is a personal record-keeping and analysis tool, intended for adults (see section 10).

2.What stays on your device

Unless you turn on Sync, everything you enter or photograph stays in your device's own storage: target photos, shot coordinates, group statistics, loads, rifles, sessions, notes, velocity readings and settings. Uninstalling the app removes it. A session file you explicitly export or share is the only other way data leaves the app.

Sample data that ships with the app is never synced. Pausing Sync or signing out stops sending immediately; changes wait on the device until you resume. The app does not send crash reports or usage statistics. If optional ones are ever added, they will be off by default and this policy will describe them before they ship.

4.How long we keep it

  • Sync data: until you delete your cloud account (see section 8), when it is removed from our servers. Records you delete in the app are kept as a small deletion marker (no content) so your other devices remove them too.
  • Data on your device: until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall.

5.Camera and photo library access

Downrange requests camera or photo-library access only when you choose to add a target photo, not on launch. Photos are used to mark shots and derive group statistics and are stored on your device. They are uploaded only if you have turned on Sync. Declining access falls back to the manual grid.

6.Subscriptions and purchases

Downrange offers an optional paid tier through Google Play's subscription system. Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing and verified using RevenueCat. When the app starts it checks your subscription status, so RevenueCat receives an app-specific user identifier and purchase and subscription-status tokens from Google Play, whether or not you have subscribed. If you use Sync, that identifier is your account's, so your subscription follows you across devices. Neither Google Play nor RevenueCat receives your target, group or load data. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

7.No sale, no advertising

We do not sell your personal information, do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and do not use it for advertising or profiling. Because of that, no "Do Not Sell or Share" link is needed under California's CCPA/CPRA or similar US state laws.

8.Your rights and controls

  • Access and portability: Settings → Export session saves your records as a JSON file. If you use Sync, you can also ask us for a copy of what is stored in your account.
  • Delete your cloud account and synced data: Settings → Sync → Delete cloud account. If you cannot open the app, use the account deletion page. Data on your device is not affected.
  • Stop syncing: pause Sync or sign out in Settings at any time.
  • Other requests (correction, objection, a complaint to your data-protection authority): email us at the address below. If you are in the EU or UK you have these rights under the GDPR; California residents have equivalent rights under the CCPA/CPRA.

9.Service providers

We use these processors under data-processing agreements: Supabase (database, storage, authentication; EU region), RevenueCat (subscriptions) and Google (Play Billing and sign-in). Each has its own sub-processors, listed in its own privacy documentation.

10.Children's privacy

Downrange is intended for people aged 18 or over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

11.Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted at this address with a revised effective date. Any new kind of data collection will be off by default and will need your opt-in.
